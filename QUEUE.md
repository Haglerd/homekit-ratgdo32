## Recently completed (log-audit-20260520-001, 2026-05-20)

- **log-audit-20260520-001** (P1) — **ACCEPTANCE MET 2026-05-22 01:33Z** — `homekitHealthTicker` callback stopped firing for 6h+ on .88 after a heap-pressure cadence transition. Root cause: the Arduino `Ticker::_attach_us()` calls `esp_timer_create()` without checking the return — under heap pressure it returns `ESP_ERR_NO_MEM`, leaves `_timer = NULL`, and subsequent `esp_timer_start_periodic(NULL, ...)` silently fails.
